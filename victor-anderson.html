St. Xavier High School

Vic was already making a name for himself as a sophomore. He ran for 1,039 yards and 27 touchdowns that season, helped St. Xavier win the Class 4A state championship, and earned MVP honors in the title game.

As a junior, he rushed for 1,576 yards and another 27 touchdowns as the Tigers returned to the state final. He topped 1,400 rushing yards and scored 22 touchdowns as a senior. He finished his high school career with 4,008 rushing yards and 75 touchdowns, and St. Xavier went 37–4 over those three seasons.

A breakout year at Louisville

Vic became Louisville’s first Big East Rookie of the Year in 2008. He ran for 1,047 yards and eight touchdowns, and The Sporting News named him a first-team freshman All-American.

His biggest game came against Kansas State, when he rushed for 176 yards and three touchdowns to earn Big East Offensive Player of the Week. Against Middle Tennessee, he ran for 161 yards, including an 88-yard touchdown. Against Connecticut, he scored both on the ground and through the air.

More than a running back

Vic made an impact as a receiver and kick returner, too. In 2010, he averaged 30.2 yards per kickoff return, seventh in the nation. As a senior, he caught 28 passes for 232 yards and a touchdown.

He was a Louisville captain for his final game against NC State. Louisville’s postgame notes credited him with 2,348 career rushing yards, ninth in school history when he finished.
