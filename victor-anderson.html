Meet your trainer

Victor Anderson

Vic played running back at St. Xavier and the University of Louisville. At Victory Sports Performance, he works with athletes on strength, speed, and conditioning.

Train with Vic

Football career

St. Xavier High School

Vic ran for 4,008 yards and scored 75 touchdowns at St. Xavier. He helped the Tigers win the Class 4A state championship as a sophomore and was named MVP of the title game.

University of Louisville

In 2008, Vic rushed for 1,047 yards and eight touchdowns, becoming Louisville’s first Big East Rookie of the Year. Against Kansas State that season, he ran for 176 yards and three touchdowns.
